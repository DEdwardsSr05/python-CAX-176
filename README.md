# python-CAX-176

Per Scholas Python Essentials (PE1) coursework.

## types_and_vars.py
Demonstrates variables, data types, and basic arithmetic/string operations.

**Example output:**
Hello my name is King Jaffe Joffer. I am 51 years of age and my height is 1.93 meters.
In 5 years I will be 56 years old.
I am trying to figure out the the area of a rectangle for my garden. The width is 5.5 meters and the length is 2 meters. This is small for agarden, but its a start.
The area is 11.0 m^2.


## simple_calculator.py
Takes two numbers and an operator (+, -, *, /), then prints the result. Handles invalid operations and division by zero.

**Example output:**
Enter the first number: 6
Enter the second number: 6
Choose an operation to perform (+, -, *, /): *
The result of 6.0 * 6.0 is: 36.0


## string_fun.py
